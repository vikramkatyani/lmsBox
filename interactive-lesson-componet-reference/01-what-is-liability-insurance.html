What is Liability Insurance?

The cover that stands behind a freight forwarder’s legal responsibilities.

3 minute readModule 1 · Page 1

Liability Insurance protects the freight forwarder, not the cargo.

When a forwarder moves goods, they take on legal responsibilities under their contract and trading conditions. If something goes wrong and the forwarder is found legally liable, this insurance responds to that liability.

It is cover for a legal obligation — not a promise to replace goods at full value.

Protects
The forwarder

Their legal liability to a customer or third party.

Triggered by
Established liability

Fault or legal responsibility must be shown first.

Governed by
Policy & contract

Wording, trading conditions and limits all apply.

“Insuring your liability is not the same as insuring the goods.”
