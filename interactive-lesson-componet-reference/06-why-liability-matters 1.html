Why does Liability Insurance matter?

Thousands of shipments move every day. Occasionally, something goes wrong.

2 minute readModule 1 · Page 6

Most consignments arrive exactly as planned. But cargo is handled, stored, lifted and driven — and incidents happen.

When they do, the financial consequences can be significant. A single damaged machine can be worth more than a year of margin on that customer’s account.

So what happens when something goes wrong?

“What happens when something goes wrong?”

Handling, storage and transit all carry risk.

Impact
Financial & commercial
