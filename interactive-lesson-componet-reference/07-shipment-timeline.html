Learning objective 06

From shipment to liability

Four stages sit between collection and any payment. Open each one to see what happens.

Select a stage to expand it

Goods are collected and move into the forwarder’s care under agreed trading conditions.

  • Contract terms are set at this point
  • Liability limits already apply
  • The customer decides whether to arrange Cargo Insurance

A road accident damages part of the consignment in transit.

  • Damage is recorded and evidenced
  • The forwarder is notified
  • No liability is assumed at this stage

The customer submits a claim for the loss they have suffered.

  • Invoices, photographs and paperwork are gathered
  • Timescales in the trading conditions apply
  • The claim is passed to the insurer

The facts are tested against the contract and the law.

  • Was the forwarder legally responsible?
  • Which limits apply to this consignment?
  • Only then can any payment be agreed

This is the step customers rarely expect — and the one that decides everything.
